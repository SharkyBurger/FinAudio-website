Karaoke word-by-word and line-by-line synced lyrics. Open a floating draggable spicy lyrics popup over your games, desktop, or mobile apps with neon glow and custom scroll modes.

Installation Tips

Windows SmartScreen notice: Because FinAudio is an indie open-source app distributed directly without a $400/year enterprise certificate, Windows SmartScreen may show a prompt. Click "More info" and select "Run anyway", or simply use the Portable (.zip) version which requires no installation.

Android APK notice: When downloading the APK, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager when prompted.

The Story

About FinAudio

Born out of a simple frustration: music apps shouldn't be loaded with unskippable ads, slow interfaces, or paid tiers just to see lyrics.

FinAudio started as a fun personal project by SharkyBurger to create the cleanest, fastest, and most expressive music experience for daily life, friends, and family.

Built with Flutter, libmpv, and modern reactive architecture, FinAudio delivers desktop-grade audio fidelity and mobile-first fluidity without compromises.

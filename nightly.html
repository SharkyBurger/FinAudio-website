Active Development

What's Being Tested Right Now

Here are the newest capabilities currently running in the Nightly channel.

Lightweight Pure Audio Downloads (~2MB - 3.5MB)

Resolves streams directly to lightweight native audio-only formats (itag 140 AAC 128k, itag 251 Opus 160k). Completely eliminates the 7MB - 12MB muxed video download bloat on PC and mobile.

Instant Hardware Audio Cutoff on Close

Low-level Windows Core Audio (WASAPI) session-muter injected directly into WM_CLOSE and window disposal. Fully eliminates lingering ~0.5s playback ring buffer leak on app exit and Alt+F4.

Album & Playlist Tracklist Inspector

Clicking an album or playlist card in search now opens the full tracklist view (AlbumViewScreen) with song numbers, durations, and metadata instead of abruptly auto-playing only track 1.

Responsive Search Grid & 1:1 Square Covers

Replaced stretched 2-column rectangular cards with an adaptive auto-fitting grid (maxCrossAxisExtent: 200), clean 1:1 square artwork thumbnails, and hovering instant-play shortcuts.

Persistent Search Navigation Stack

Returning back from an inspected album preserves your active search query, category filter pill (e.g. Albums), and loaded results without resetting back to an empty screen.

Lightweight ~2MB AAC Offline Music

High-efficiency pure-Dart AAC demuxing cuts storage requirements from 12MB+ to ~2.2MB per track. Streamlined download options to Normal (128 kbps) and High (256/320 kbps).

HD Animated GIF Preset Wallpapers

All curated theme presets feature bundled 1366×768 animated backdrops (Midnight Ocean, Pure OLED, Lemon Shark, Emerald Cyber, Sunset Glow, Neon Pink, Slate, Ellen Joe) with seamless gapless looping.
